# GripLock

## https://griplock.windnoise.org/

Paint where and how hard a grip presses on a Glock 17, paint the finger on the trigger, and fire. GripLock replays the
recoil in slow motion with its numbers on the gun, and shows where the shot lands on a target down the range. Change
the paint and fire again.

It simulates the force on the gun alone: each painted spot presses into the gun like flesh and holds it by friction,
the trigger breaks on how hard the painted finger turns it, and the slide, barrel and recoil spring move as one
mechanism. A Fire can be a string of up to 17 shots, and a replay saves as an MP4 video. Every number the simulation
uses, and where it comes from, is on its numbers page.

It runs in a current desktop browser (tested in Chrome) and needs an internet connection for its 3D library and fonts.
Your paint and saved grips stay in your browser; the app sends none of them anywhere.
