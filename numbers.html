The numbers behind GripLock

Every physical number the app uses, read here from the app's own data files, so this page cannot disagree with the app. Estimates are reasoned, not taken from a source: each says what it leans on, and each could be measured.

Not listed, because they are not yet records with a note each: the gun's mass layout inside the frame and the small forces that resist the slide (stripping a round, cocking the striker, the rails), all estimates written as plain numbers in data/glock17.js, carried over from an earlier version.
